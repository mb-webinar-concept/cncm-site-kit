# 01 · Refresh Local Impact statistics

**Page:** https://www.childnutritioncouncil.com/local-impact
**Why:** the page cites food-insecurity figures from 2022 and a fruit-and-vegetable figure from 2015. Newer figures were published in April 2026.

## Current text
> 1 in 4 children under 18 in Manitoba live in a food insecure household (StatCan, 2022)

## New text
> In 2025, 27.9% of Manitobans lived in food-insecure households, the third-highest rate among the provinces, and Manitoba was the only province where the rate did not decline. (PROOF, University of Toronto, 2026)

Optional second line:
> Across Canada, 30.8% of children under 18 lived in food-insecure households in 2025. (PROOF, University of Toronto, 2026)

Link "PROOF, University of Toronto, 2026" to https://proof.utoronto.ca/?p=8132

## The 2015 figure
> Over one third of students reported they do not always consume a fruit or vegetable in a day (Health Canada, 2015)

No newer source was found. CNCM decides: keep it with its year, or remove it.

## Steps
1. Log in to Squarespace, open **Local Impact**, click **Edit**.
2. Click the text block containing the current text and replace it with the new text. Keep the existing styling.
3. Select "PROOF, University of Toronto, 2026" and add the link.
4. **Save**.

## Check
Open the page in a private window, logged out: the new figure shows and the link opens PROOF's article.
