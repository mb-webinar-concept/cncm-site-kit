# Local Impact page: refreshed statistics

The page currently cites figures from 2022 (Statistics Canada) and 2015 (Health Canada). Suggested replacements, with sources CNCM can link to.

## Replace

> 1 in 4 children under 18 in Manitoba live in a food insecure household (StatCan, 2022)

## With

> In 2025, 27.9% of Manitobans lived in food-insecure households, the third-highest rate among the provinces, and Manitoba was the only province where the rate did not decline.
> Source: PROOF, University of Toronto, April 2026 — https://proof.utoronto.ca/?p=8132

> Across Canada, 30.8% of children under 18, almost 2.4 million, lived in food-insecure households in 2025.
> Source: PROOF, University of Toronto, April 2026 — https://proof.utoronto.ca/?p=8132

## Keep or remove

> Over one third of students reported they do not always consume a fruit or vegetable in a day (Health Canada, 2015)

We did not find a newer source for this figure. Either keep it with its year shown, or remove it.

## Add (CNCM's own latest numbers)

> In 2025–26, CNCM granted $4.7 million to 443 school food programs, serving more than 81,000 students every school day.
> Source: CNCM Annual Report 2025–26

Note: the regional totals on the page add up to 421 schools, while the annual report counts 443 programs. Programs and schools are not the same count; consider labelling the regional list "schools" and stating its date.
