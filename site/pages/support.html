Program support

Every funded program has its own registered dietitian.

What your dietitian does

  • Visits, calls and emails to help with menus, budgets and running the program.
  • A yearly review of your grocery receipts, with comments to guide menu planning.
  • Workshops, webinars and networking calls with other programs.
