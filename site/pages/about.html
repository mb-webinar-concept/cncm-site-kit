About us

Every child, every day, well nourished.

What we do

The Child Nutrition Council of Manitoba is a registered charity founded in 2001. We give grants to schools for meal and snack programs that are open to every student at no cost, and our registered dietitians help each program run well.

The Manitoba School Boards Association was a founding member and still supports our work. We are part of the Coalition for Healthy School Food, the national network for school food.
