Next

Apply for grants online

Apply, save drafts, attach documents and see where your application stands. Needs a privacy review and a choice of tool.

Later

Upload receipts

Programs send receipts securely; dietitians review them in one queue and the report comes back in the same place. Today 136 programs' receipts are reviewed by hand.

Later

Your program dashboard

One sign-in for a school: grant status, instalments, report deadlines, your dietitian and recommended resources.

Register for upcoming webinars

Connected grants and finance

CNCM's finances run through a financial officer at the Manitoba School Boards Association. A later stage could link grant decisions, instalments and receipts to that finance system so figures are entered once. Scope to be set with MSBA's finance office.

Needs a decision

Payroll and expenses online

Staff time and expense claims submitted online and passed to the finance system, if MSBA's systems allow it.
