Grants

Funding for school meal and snack programs open to every student.

2026–27 applications are closed

We are not accepting new applications for 2026–27. Schools can join the waitlist.

Join the waitlist on our current site Apply online: coming later

Who can apply

  • Programs in Manitoba for school-age students enrolled in an education program.
  • Running during school hours at least two days a week, for regular nourishment rather than one-time events.
  • Open to every student at no cost, whatever their circumstances.
  • With the space, time and commitment to follow the Manitoba School Nutrition Guidelines.

Adult learning, after-school, early learning and child care programs are not eligible. We fund part of each program, so schools also look for support from their community.
