Program support

Every funded program has its own registered dietitian.

What your dietitian does

  • Visits, calls and emails to help with menus, budgets and running the program.
  • A yearly review of your grocery receipts, with comments to guide menu planning.
  • Workshops, webinars and networking calls with other programs.
  • 265 contactsVisits and calls with programs in 2025–26
  • 151 peopleFrom 98 schools attended 6 workshops
  • 189 peopleFrom 110 schools attended 5 webinars
  • 136 programsReceived a receipt review

Source: CNCM Annual Report 2025–26. Contacts: 125 in-person and 84 phone or video one-on-one, 56 small-group visits.
